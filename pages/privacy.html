Privacy Policy

How Orvexa Global Tech collects, uses, and safeguards personal information across our platforms and services.

Home / Privacy Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

At Orvexa Global Tech ("Orvexa", "we", "our", or "us"), we prioritize the privacy and security of our clients, website visitors, and SaaS platform users. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles and practices concerning the collection, storage, processing, and disclosure of your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information directly from you or automatically when you interact with our website and software products:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, work email address, telephone number, corporate entity name, job designation.
  • Project Inquiries: Technical specifications, business objectives, and budget allocations submitted via our contact and demo forms.
  • Automated Telemetry: IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, session timestamps, and page interaction metrics collected via cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is utilized strictly for legitimate business and engineering purposes, including:

  • Responding to service consultations, proposal requests, and demo bookings.
  • Provisioning, maintaining, and securing our multi-tenant SaaS environments.
  • Complying with contractual obligations and industry regulatory standards (GDPR, SOC2).
  • Improving our platform performance, user interfaces, and cybersecurity defenses.

4. Data Protection & Cybersecurity

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest), zero-trust access controls, regular vulnerability assessments, and strict multi-factor authentication to ensure your data remains protected against unauthorized access.

5. Your Data Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by Orvexa Global Tech. To exercise these rights, please contact our Data Governance Officer at privacy@orvexaglobaltech.com.
