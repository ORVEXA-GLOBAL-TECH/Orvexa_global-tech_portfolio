Project 01 Enterprise SaaS

Enterprise Management Platform

Challenge: Fragmented legacy ERP architecture causing multi-hour inventory delays, data inconsistency, and inability to handle seasonal traffic surges.

Solution: Engineered a resilient cloud microservices architecture with real-time distributed caching, asynchronous queueing, and modular multi-entity ledger.

Technology: Java, Spring Boot, React, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, AWS ECS.

Result: 42% acceleration in warehouse processing, zero data loss, and 99.99% continuous system uptime.

Project 02 HealthTech

Healthcare Management System

Challenge: Disconnected clinical patient records, manual doctor scheduling errors, and strict HIPAA compliance requirements.

Solution: Built an end-to-end encrypted telehealth and EHR platform with automated appointment dispatch, smart calendar sync, and audit trails.

Technology: Python, FastAPI, Next.js, PostgreSQL, WebRTC, Azure Cloud.

Result: 60% reduction in patient intake waiting times, 100% HIPAA audit compliance, and seamless cross-clinic sync.

Project 03 AI & Machine Learning

AI-Powered Platform

Challenge: Enterprise customer support overwhelmed by repetitive inquiries and lengthy document verification queues.

Solution: Implemented a multi-agent RAG workflow with semantic embedding search, contextual guardrails, and auto-escalation to human reps.

Technology: Python, LangChain, OpenAI, Pinecone Vector DB, Redis, React.

Result: 68% autonomous ticket resolution rate, average response time reduced from 4 hours to under 30 seconds.

Project 04 Automation & FinTech

Business Automation System

Challenge: Manual reconciliation of 50,000+ monthly vendor invoices prone to human error and accounting delays.

Solution: Developed an automated OCR extraction, 3-way matching, and ERP ledger posting automation engine.

Technology: Node.js, Python OCR, MongoDB, AWS Lambda, RabbitMQ.

Result: 85% reduction in manual billing processing hours and zero reconciliation discrepancies.
