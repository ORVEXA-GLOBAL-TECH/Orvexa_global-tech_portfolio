Cookie Policy

How and why Orvexa Global Tech uses cookies and tracking technologies across our web interfaces.

Home / Cookie Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Compliant with: ePrivacy Directive & GDPR

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to ensure websites function efficiently, improve user experience, and provide anonymous operational analytics to website operators.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for user session security, CSRF protection, and navigation stability across our SaaS portal.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Anonymous telemetry (e.g. page load duration, broken link detection) that helps us optimize software responsiveness.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your interface preferences such as language, theme toggles, and form state restoration.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can modify your browser settings to decline or delete cookies at any time. Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may impact the accessibility of certain secure areas in our SaaS products.
